## 2026-05-10 - Offer Copy Action and Clipboard Feedback Guarding
**Learning:** When providing a one-click copy button for multiline text snippets (e.g. outreach offers) in dense data tables, users often click multiple times if they do not receive immediate tactile/visual feedback. Guarding the copy state with `dataset.isCopying` prevents race conditions with active `setTimeout` resets, and toggling accessible text alongside an updated `aria-label` ensures both visual users and screen reader users are informed when copying succeeds without needing separate toast notifications.
**Action:** Always include a guard flag (`dataset.isCopying`) and update both text content and `aria-label` when designing inline copy buttons, pairing with high-contrast `:focus-visible` outlines for keyboard users.
