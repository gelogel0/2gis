## 2025-05-15 - [Copy to Clipboard Pattern]
**Learning:** For a B2B outreach tool where users frequently interact with generated text, a 'Copy to Clipboard' button with immediate visual feedback (icon change and ARIA label update) significantly reduces friction and provides clear confirmation of success.
**Action:** Implement 'Copy' buttons for all long-form generated content (like offers or bios) and ensure they follow the checkmark-feedback pattern with accessible labels.
